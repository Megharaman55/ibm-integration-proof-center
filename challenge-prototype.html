IBM Integration / Internal Stakeholder Brief / Leadership & PMM Strategy
IBM INTERNAL USE ONLY • STRATEGY & EXPERIMENT

Proof-led conversion: hypothesis and measurement

Testing an outcome-oriented technical proof journey as an alternative conversion model for high-value IBM Integration prospects.

STRATEGIC CONVERSION HYPOTHESIS

Why outcome-oriented technical proof de-risks enterprise buyer journeys

For qualified IBM Integration prospects (integration architects, engineering directors, and modernization leads), an open-ended generic "Start free trial" often introduces friction and high drop-off before first value is realized. High-value enterprise buyers frequently do not have time to configure an empty sandbox from scratch without sample data, clear guardrails, or organizational alignment.

Core Hypothesis: Presenting three outcome-oriented technical proof paths alongside traditional live demo options will increase qualified buyer progression, shorten sales cycles, and provide pre-sales engineering teams with structured, evidence-based engagement scopes rather than open-ended support requests.
